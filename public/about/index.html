About KopelAi

A private space for therapists to reflect.

What it is

KopelAi is a private, ongoing conversation that helps therapists understand themselves as clinicians - their strengths, their blind spots, and the patterns they carry into the room. It listens, reflects, and challenges through a psychoanalytic lens, and over time it surfaces what keeps coming up. It is a tool for personal reflection - not therapy, and not formal supervision.
